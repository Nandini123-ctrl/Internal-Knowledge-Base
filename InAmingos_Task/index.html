About Our NGO

InAmigos Foundation is a youth-driven non-profit organization dedicated to creating a positive impact in society. We believe that small acts of kindness, when multiplied by many people, can transform the world. Our core mission is to bridge gaps in education, healthcare, environmental protection, and community welfare.

Our Ongoing Projects

Project Bachpanshala

Focuses on providing quality education, learning materials, and digital literacy support to underprivileged children.

Project Seva

Distributing food packets, essential clothing, and relief kits to homeless and daily-wage communities.

Project Prakriti

Promoting environmental sustainability through massive tree plantation drives and cleanliness awareness campaigns.

Our Social Impact

Through our dedicated campaigns and the hard work of our interns and volunteers, we have touched hundreds of lives. Our projects focus on sustainable development, ensuring long-term upliftment rather than just temporary aid.
